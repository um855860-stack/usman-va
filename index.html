A bit about how I work

I help with the parts of an Amazon store that are easy to let pile up: product research, listings, ad campaigns, inventory checks and everyday Seller Central tasks. The goal is to make the work feel more manageable, not more complicated.

I like to understand the store before suggesting changes. I’ll explain what I find, be upfront about what still needs checking, and focus on next steps that suit your goals and budget. No overnight-success promises—just careful work and clear updates.

Clear updates, no guesswork You’ll know what I’m working on, what I’ve found and what I recommend next. If something needs a closer look, I’ll say so.
