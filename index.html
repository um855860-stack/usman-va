A Little About Me

I work best with people who want their Amazon business to feel more organized, not more complicated. My role is to bring structure to the details that are easy to miss: product research, listing quality, campaign decisions, inventory, and the small Seller Central tasks that keep a store moving.

I do not believe in promising overnight results. I prefer to understand the store first, explain what I am seeing, and work through improvements that make sense for the brand and its budget. You can expect clear updates, thoughtful research, and someone who takes ownership of the work.

What working with me feels like Calm communication, practical recommendations, and consistent follow-through. I will tell you what I know, what I still need to check, and what I think should happen next.
